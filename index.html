About Me

I am Xueyi Chen, an incoming PhD student at Westlake University starting in Fall 2026 advised by Prof. Huan Wang, and currently a research intern at the ENCODE Lab. Previously, I earned my MSc in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) in 2025 supervised by Prof. Bei Yu and my BEng in Software Engineering from Fuzhou University in 2024.

Research Interests

I am broadly interested in building efficient and capable AI systems:

  • Multimodal Understanding [StreamingTOM] [LVOmniBench]
  • LLM for Kernel Generation [DICE] [MobileKernelBench]
  • RL for LLM Reasoning [OORL] [ToTRL]

I am actively seeking research internships and always open to potential collaborations. Please feel free to contact me at xueyi.chen.2024@gmail.com or chenxueyi@westlake.edu.cn.
