About Me

I am Xueyi Chen, a first-year PhD student at Westlake University, advised by Prof. Huan Wang at the ENCODE Lab. I am currently a research intern at inclusionAI, Ant Group. Previously, I earned my MSc in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) in 2025, supervised by Prof. Bei Yu, and my BEng in Software Engineering from Fuzhou University in 2024.

Research Interests

I am broadly interested in building efficient and capable AI systems:

  • Multimodal Understanding [StreamingTOM] [LVOmniBench]
  • LLMs for Kernel Generation [MetaKernelBench]
  • RL for LLM Reasoning [OORL] [ToTRL]

I am always open to potential collaborations. Please feel free to contact me at xueyi.chen.2024@gmail.com or chenxueyi@westlake.edu.cn.
