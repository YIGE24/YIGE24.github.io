📄 We released **LVOmniBench**, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating omnimodal LLMs on long audio-video understanding! This work was led by co-first authors [Keda Tao](https://kd-tao.github.io/) and [Yuhua Zheng](https://pillor9.github.io/). Check out our [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19217), [project page](https://kd-tao.github.io/LVOmniBench/), and [code](https://github.com/KD-TAO/LVOmniBench)!
